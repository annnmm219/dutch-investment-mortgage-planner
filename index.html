Box 3 assumptions and advanced settings

2026 current rules, investment-only estimate

For a portfolio consisting only of ordinary investments. The statutory calculation uses the 2026 tax-free wealth allowance and deemed investment return. The calculator also models the actual-return rebuttal and uses the lower modeled tax. Under the actual-return route, the tax-free wealth allowance does not apply.

Official 2026 amount: €59,357 per person, or €118,714 for two fiscal partners.

2026 investments and other assets: 6.00%.

Needed when the plan starts after January.

The 2026 proxy is held constant for 2027 because final 2027 parameters are not yet available in this planning model.

Model status and sources
Mortgage structure reference: WhatTheMortgage.com for its annuity/linear comparator and monthly Balance / Gross / Capital / Interest / Tax Return / Net breakdown. Calculations here are independently implemented for an existing mortgage and life-phase planning.
Mortgage deduction: Belastingdienst 2026 mortgage-interest deduction limitation and eigenwoningforfait rules.
Current Box 3: Belastingdienst 2026 rate, €59,357 tax-free wealth per person, and 6.00% deemed return for investments/other assets; the actual-return rebuttal includes value changes and does not use the tax-free wealth allowance.
Proposed Box 3: Rijksoverheid / parliamentary bill Wet werkelijk rendement box 3. Ordinary listed shares and funds/ETFs are modeled using annual wealth accretion; current bill parameters remain editable in the Investment section.
